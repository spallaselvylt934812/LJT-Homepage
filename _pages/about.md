I am a **first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group** at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, supervised by **Professor Junxian He**. My research focuses on **natural language processing (NLP)** and **machine learning (ML)**.

I completed my undergraduate studies at **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)**, graduating in June 2024 with a **B.Eng.** degree (2020–2024). During my time at SJTU I was previously advised by Professor Junxian He, and I received the **Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship**.

My research interests lie in:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
